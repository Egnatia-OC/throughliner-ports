#### /setup creates two exposures — the commit identity and the planning record — and names neither at the moment publishing is decided [setup-creates-unnamed-public-exposures]

#### Shell commands bypass the file scope-lock entirely — `pre_tool_use.py` only guards Edit/Write/MultiEdit [shell-writes-bypass-file-scope-lock]
By Claude, from this session's scope-lock audit; filed at your request.
