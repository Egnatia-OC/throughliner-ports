#### Build the light 5-series docset (Fable 5 + Opus 5) + session-start model detection; freeze the 4.8 docset as fallback [fable-docset-model-detection]
Captured by you; fork resolved and reframed 2026-07-31 via research at resources/research/opus-5-instruction-compliance.md. Opus 5 shipped 2026-07-24, which is what let us settle the long-open strategic gate.
**The fork, resolved.** Opus 5 is *not* "as fussy as 4.8" — it's fussy in the opposite direction. 4.8 under-steers (literal; needs explicit scope and a why-clause riding every rule or it slips — which is why docset A is heavy). Opus 5 over-does (self-verifies, expands scope, verbose by default); Anthropic's guidance for it is subtraction, not addition. Fable 5's guidance says the same — over-prescription degrades it. **Three ordered phases (a future /plan splits these into their own build lines when this lifts):**
