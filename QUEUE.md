# QUEUE

Two sections. **Processed** — work discussed and agreed, ordered top-to-bottom; /next builds from above the `--- Cleared to run above this line ---` marker. **Unprocessed** — captured, not yet discussed; the next /plan weighs each item. A work line is a `#### ` heading (its description) with a `[slug]` at the end of that line and its rationale in the block beneath. A leading `[audit]` / `[user]` tag names how it's executed; no tag means a build. Red flags are ordinary work lines carrying a `Red flag · State: …` marker.

#### Red-flag lifecycle at ship: a flag must ride real work, and its resolution is forced and recorded at close [red-flag-ship-lifecycle]
By Claude. Folds in [redflag-standalone-tracking-line] and [redflag-resolution-not-forced-at-ship] — two halves of one question: what happens to a red flag when its work-line completes. One half caught a flag lingering as a standalone workless line ([device-access-consent] above, whose fix already shipped and whose only remaining step — live verification — lives on a different `[user]` line); the other caught a flag vanishing unrecorded when its line shipped ([consumer-plugin-feedback-channel] at its close). Both violate the redesign's model, where a red flag is only a marker on a line that carries real work — never a pinned tracking line, never a silent disappearance.

The rule to build: a red-flag marker must always sit on a line carrying real remaining work. At the moment a red-flag-carrying line's work completes, the close forces an explicit resolve/accept decision and records it in the LOG before the line leaves the queue. A completed fix flips to **resolved** immediately — the code no longer carries the risk, which is what "resolved" means; live verification then rides the ordinary `[user]` verification line as a normal check, not as an open flag. A flag may neither linger as a workless standalone line nor vanish unrecorded.

Build touches: plugin-behaviour.md (Red flags — the lifecycle rule), the close routing in done.md / done-build.md (force-and-record the decision before a red-flag line leaves), and a faq-template.md entry (+ index line) since a consumer meets this at close. Immediate cleanup folded in: mark [device-access-consent] **resolved** and remove the standalone line, recording the resolution in LOG; confirm [consumer-plugin-feedback-channel]'s flag is recorded resolved; audit the remaining red-flag lines for the same shape. Resolving [device-access-consent] also clears one of the two flags currently gating /cruise.
