#### A two-hook relay may give compaction re-injection the trigger it was said not to have [compaction-reinjection-via-postcompact-flag]

**Both facts the audit was asked for, read from `code.claude.com/docs/en/hooks` rather than inferred.** **(a)** The set of events that can return `hookSpecificOutput.additionalContext` still includes `UserPromptSubmit` and `PostToolUse`, and still excludes `PreCompact`, `PostCompact` and `SessionEnd`.
