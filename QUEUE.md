Each is load-bearing the first time someone proposes reversing it, and paid on every close forever by sessions that were never going to propose anything. #### Superseded research leaves every item scoped against it silently wrong, and nothing connects the two [no-link-from-research-to-items-scoped-on-it]

#### The filing-claim hook blocks on captures presented as drafts for approval, which is a shape the method specifies [stop-hook-fires-on-drafted-not-filed-captures]
Filed 2026-08-12 by Claude at the /plan wind-down, from a live block moments earlier in the same session.
**What happened.** The wind-down presented four fully-drafted candidate captures, each with its slug, as one numbered set for a single approval — the shape `plan.md`'s wind-down re-scan and the bulk-approval inversion in `skill-nonspecific-rules.md` both specify. No write had happened and none was claimed. The Stop hook read the slugs as claims that the items had been filed, found them absent from QUEUE.md, and blocked the turn.
**The hook is behaving as designed, which is what makes this worth filing.** It checks a finished response for a report naming a work item as filed and confirms the item exists. Its stated purpose is to stop a user acting on a report of work that does not exist. **The cost is bounded and real.** `SPEC.md` records that the hook blocks once per claim and then stops, so it cannot trap the conversation, and the turn recovered. What it produces is a wrong signal at a correct moment, plus a message telling Claude to make a write the user has not yet approved — which, if followed literally, would write four unapproved items into the queue and invert the approval the wind-down exists to collect.
**To settle at processing.** Whether the hook can distinguish a draft from a report at all — the honest answer may be no, since both name a slug in prose. Options: exempt a response that presents multiple unfiled slugs alongside an explicit approval question; have the wind-down present drafts without slugs, assigning them at filing (cheap, and slugs are assigned at filing anyway per the Captures rules); or accept the false positive and make the hook's message say that a draft awaiting approval is a legitimate case and no write should be made. The second is cheapest and touches only `plan.md`.
**Files (rough):** `plugin/si-plugin/hooks/stop.py` and/or `plugin/si-plugin/docs-b/plan.md` (the wind-down's presentation shape). Shipped either way. Relates to [write-first-report-without-write] (the item that built this hook).
