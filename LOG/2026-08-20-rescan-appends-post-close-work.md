# b485ee3 — /rescan widened to route by the three-way triage, so a post-close tail needs one word

Captured by the user immediately after [done-delta-close] was deleted — and it is not that item. Her framing: a skill so she does not have to type the whole request and the reasoning behind it every time; it picks up the post-close work, logs it, and it rides the next commit.

What existed, and what did not. `/rescan` was already user-invoked, repeatable within a chat, scanning back only as far as the last `/rescan`, filing to Unprocessed and committing nothing. What it did not do is touch the session's LOG entry: `rescan.md` named `LOG/` once, and only to place a capture. So a conversation's *decisions* had a one-word route and post-close *work* had none.

**Why this survives the refusal that killed [done-delta-close].** That refusal was against a second commit wearing another name, and against needing someone to judge when the tail had ended. Neither applies: this commits nothing, and it can be run as many times as the tail has parts. Both halves are now written into the doc, so a later session meets the argument rather than re-deriving it.

/rescan now routes what it finds by the three-way triage the always-loaded rules already carry, rather than filing captures only: work to do becomes a capture, and what already *happened* becomes an append to this session's LOG entry as a marked tail. Its stated boundary is untouched — it files, and never decides a fate or builds. Two cases are written out because they are the ones a session will meet and improvise around: a chat with no LOG entry yet has nothing to append to, and a candidate that is genuinely both gets both, rather than one standing in for the other.

**One consequence in `done.md`:** its paragraph titled "Why this is not covered by /rescan" became false the moment this shipped, and was rewritten rather than left standing. What that section actually covers is the same class arriving at the close rather than on demand — the close always runs, /rescan only when asked. The one-commit table gained a /rescan row for the same reason.

**Files touched:** `plugin/throughliner/docs-b/rescan.md` (the routing), `done.md` (the coordinating paragraph and the one-commit table), `faq-template.md` and `faq-index-template.md` with their `FAQ/` copies. SPEC.md is not listed — its `/rescan` bullet was rewritten in the planning session that kept this.
