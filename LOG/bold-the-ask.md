# 29ba751 - plugin-behaviour.md: render the single user-facing ask in bold, phrased as a question

The lead-with-the-decision and approval-time-ask rules make the ask exist and come first, but do not make it findable in a long message, and a message cannot always be short. A new Communication rule renders the single user-facing ask in bold and as a question, so a reader who struggles to locate it in a wall of text always can - a findability aid and an accessibility one. The rationale travels with the rule inline, because Opus 4.8 applies a rule far more reliably when its why is attached. It complements, not replaces, one-item-at-a-time and lead-with-the-decision.
