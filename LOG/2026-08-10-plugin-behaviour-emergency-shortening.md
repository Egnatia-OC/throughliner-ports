Then the session's largest outcome, which is not an edit at all. Asked at the wind-down whether anything discussed had gone unfiled, the answer surfaced her earlier aside that she was considering retiring the doc entirely — and drafting that capture drew out the argument. Two causes, both hers. The doc's *title* is doing the filing: she is confident that when rules get authored, Claude infers from the name that all plugin behaviour rules belong there, which is why rules rarely land anywhere else and why this one file bloats while its siblings do not. And the founding premise fails on arithmetic — stating a rule once was meant to save context against repeating it, but a rule repeated in skill-specific docs is skill-specific, so those copies are paid only when that skill runs while an always-loaded rule is paid every session. The consolidation therefore trades a sometimes-cost for an always-cost, which is backwards. Her own account of the original decision is that she was thinking less prose = better and did not go deeper. Her call: it's time for this doc to go, chiefly on that arithmetic. Filed as [retire-plugin-behaviour-doc], which if built supersedes several of the eviction items still queued — including parts of what this very session executed.
