# cb50e2b — the decay rung reaches nothing in practice; interleaving adopted for the rest of the session, and it surfaced a 17-day-old item on its first pick

Raised by the user at the end of a long session, in her own words: "you can stay stuck on size forever, never reaching the decay rung by the end of any plan session." She said the thought had been complete earlier and she was running on empty by the time she got to share it. It landed on a real defect anyway.

She is right and the arithmetic is the argument. Rung 3 was partitioned at the section's median line count so that it terminates, which fixed a genuine formal defect — a rung that ranked every entry and so never yielded, making anything beneath it unreachable by construction. It did not fix the practical problem. The above-median group is by definition half the section, roughly 16 items in Unprocessed, and reaching rung 4 means processing all of them in one session. This session processed seven. So decay is reachable in principle and never at any realistic session length.

Her proposed fix was refused and the reason recorded, because it is the intuitive one. Combining the two rungs as "date by size" or "size by date" is a lexicographic order: one key dominates and the other only breaks ties, so whichever goes first the second starves exactly as decay starves now. The ordering is relabelled rather than repaired.

Two fixes work and only one avoids inventing a figure. A composite score mixing age and size needs weights, and a weight is a bare number with no derivation, which this project bans — the same objection kills any ageing rate, and it is the objection that retired the word bands in this same session. Interleaving needs neither: alternate between the above-median group and the oldest-filed item, one from each in turn. Starvation becomes impossible by construction rather than merely unlikely, every pick still reads a field the digest already computes, and nothing is written into the method's text. What it costs is real and stated: longest-first exists to shrink the queue fastest, and alternating halves that rate — half the benefit of a rung that works against the full benefit of a rung that is never reached.

She then asked to run the interleave for the rest of the session, which makes this session the check the capture said it needed before the change can be kept. One correction was made in applying it: the interleave replaces the bottom two rungs, not the ladder, so unblock-potential still selects first — and it selected the one item that also satisfied the session's throughput floor.
