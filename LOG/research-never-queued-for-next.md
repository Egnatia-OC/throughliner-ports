# [HASH] — Research is never queued for /next: it can only produce knowledge, so it happens in the /plan that needs it

The user's rule, raised while processing a different item whose draft carried "research first" as its opening step: research must never be planned as next-work, because its only output is findings — captures, corrected premises, informed designs — never a build. Queuing it defers the very information the planning discussion needed, so the item gets designed on an unverified premise and the research lands later as a stale errand.

Checked against the shipped docs before building rather than assumed: plan.md already carries the resolve-now-versus-capture block, and /next has no research flavor at all — the flavors are build, `[audit]` and `[user]`. So research already has no legitimate route into a run, and what was actually missing is **the tell** and **the boundary**, not a new prohibition. That made this a cheap sharpening of text already leaning the right way.

**The tell: a work item whose first step is "research X" is the sign that /plan skipped its own job.**

Both open questions resolve, and both answers went into the rule.

**Measurement infrastructure is not research.** A harness builds a runner, a scenario set and checkers — files that exist when it finishes. The discriminator, stated plainly: **does anything survive the work except knowledge?** If yes it is a build, whatever its subject. That keeps the rule from swallowing every investigative-sounding build item.

**A genuinely multi-session investigation stays in Unprocessed, and its *processing* is the research** — across as many /plan sessions as it takes. It never becomes /next work and needs no new container.

**Naming that second answer was not optional.** The method's own recorded pattern is that a prohibition at a moment of real pressure reliably produces an invented escape route, and an invented move is worse than the thing prohibited because nothing recognises or records it. Three separate rules have been walked into for exactly this reason. "Never queue research" creates precisely that pressure — the research is real and has to go somewhere — so the legitimate home is stated in the same breath as the prohibition.

No FAQ entry, and the reason is recorded rather than left as a silent skip: the user never meets this rule. What they experience is research happening in the planning session that needed it instead of being deferred, which needs no explanation.
