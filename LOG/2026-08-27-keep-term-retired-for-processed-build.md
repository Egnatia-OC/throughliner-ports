# 32675a3 — "Keep" retires as the disposition term; an entry is processed, with its outcome named

"Keep" said nothing about where a piece of work went. It named the operation by
its opposite — not-delete — and the queue has two destinations behind it: cleared
to run, or held below the line. So the word made the user ask a follow-up question
every time, and "keep-step" named the step after the vaguer of the two answers it
produces.

Processing an entry now ends in one of three named outcomes: **into Processed,
cleared to run**; **into Processed, held below the line**; or **deleted**. The
recommendation names the one it proposes, and the checkpoint reports where the
last entry landed in those words — "Into Processed, cleared to run." rather than
"Kept and cleared."

**The step is now the decision step.** The build chose the name, as the item asked
it to: "disposition" was the obvious candidate and is the word that had to be
explained to the user in the session that decided this, which disqualified it.

**Blind substitution was refused**, and this is where the work was. Most "keep"
occurrences in these docs are ordinary English — keep-private, "keeps its own
reply", "keep discussing" — and a global replace would have mangled them. Each
site was judged.

**Rewording old "Kept" stamps in QUEUE.md and LOG/ was refused**: records keep the
vocabulary of their time.

**Files touched:** `plugin/throughliner/docs/plan.md` (the recommend table, the
execute step, the fold table, the checkpoint specimen and message order, and eight
further term-of-art sites), `next.md`, `next-build.md`, `done.md`,
`feedback-and-inbox.md`, `migrate-checklist.md`, `skill-nonspecific-rules.md`,
`SPEC.md`, `CLAUDE.md`. A grep confirms no shipped doc, template, FAQ copy, SPEC
or CLAUDE.md uses "keep" as the disposition term or "keep-step" as the step name.

**Scope note, stated rather than buried:** `feedback-and-inbox.md` and
`migrate-checklist.md` were added to the run's file list mid-item. Both carried
cross-doc references to the renamed step, which the item's own instructions
required updating — the item under-enumerated its files rather than this being new
scope.

**Routed to Captures:** none.

Retired: `keep` / `Kept` / `keep-step` / `keep/delete` / `keep-check` — the disposition vocabulary and the step's name. Recorded in `resources/retired-terms.md` as the compounds only, never as the bare word, for the same cry-wolf reason as the entry beside it.

Rule gate: run — terminology correction under the shared-vocabulary supersession already gated on [shared-vocabulary-not-standing-names]; no new rule.
