# [HASH] — every word-band cap retired, the measurement kept as a report, and four cleared items stripped of the figures they rested on

The user's decision, in her own words: the numbered caps are "too arbitrary and we have realer leads now". She had said a few turns earlier that she was disillusioned with ceilings on item size, and the disillusionment turned out to have harder support than either of us had stated.

The argument that settled it came from the measuring tool. `measure_written_shape_length.py`'s own docstring refuses to print a band beside a distribution, because "a band printed inside the distribution report would be a threshold read off the thing being questioned". The shipped bands are exactly that — the middle of what this corpus already wrote. The script declines to do the thing the rules did. This project had also already accepted that argument one layer up: the rule-corpus ceiling was removed on the ground that it had lost its derivation and no defensible replacement threshold existed, and `rule_signals.py` still carries the reasoning and reports growth with no verdict. The word bands were the surviving instance of a pattern already retired.

Three facts stood behind it rather than a mood. The rules' own caveat calls the figures lengths demonstrated to be sufficient, never ideal — a description of past behaviour, not a standard. The re-derivation the day before had to exclude three shapes of five for want of defensible data, including work items, which is the shape she kept breaching: 345 is 229 × 1.5, and 229 is July's median, so the number she was chafing against was the last one still made of invented multipliers. And acting on a breach has a measured yield of 8%, 3% and 9% across three passes.

The replacement had to be neither figures nor prose. A prose instruction to be concise shipped first and measurably did nothing, which is the recorded reason the bands became figures at all — so retiring figures back to prose walks into a known failure. What replaces them is structure: the build block and relocation along the seam it creates. That is untried, and it is the first lever aimed at where text lives rather than how it is worded.

What is kept is the posture this project already trusts everywhere else: measure, never cap. The script still ships and prints distributions with no thresholds, which is what the queue digest does and what the queue-growth hook says in its own output — bare facts, no threshold, no judgment. What also survives is the position rather than the arithmetic: both failures are real, a record too thin to rebuild intent from and one too long to get through, and length still has to be earned. Only the claim to have located that line to within a word count goes.

The eviction rippled into four items already cleared to run, each amended rather than left to contradict the retirement. [word-band-script-does-not-ship] keeps the shipping defect it was filed for and loses the shipping of the figures. [split-action-defeats-the-bands-in-aggregate] is stronger for it: its real justification was never the 20–40 figure but that `LOG/index.md` is read in full on every retrieve — 48,165 words of pointer, one 337-word line pointing at a 1,710-word entry — so the test becomes the index line's own contract, which was always the real one with a number standing in front of it. [tersify-on-request-not-at-the-close] loses the breach report it was going to give the close, there being no breach left to report. And [measurement-audits-for-the-remaining-shapes] dissolved entirely and was deleted: it existed to replace three sets of invented figures with derived ones, and there were none left to replace.
