# b485ee3 — The ordering ladder's bottom two rungs merged into one that can actually be reached

Raised by the user, in her own words: *"you can stay stuck on size forever, never reaching the decay rung by the end of any plan session."* The arithmetic is the argument. Rung 3 had been partitioned at the section's median line count so that it terminates, which fixed the formal defect — a rung that ranked every entry and so never yielded. It did not fix the practical one: the above-median group is by construction **half the section**, and reaching rung 4 meant processing all of it in one session. Sessions process a handful. So the decay rung was reachable in principle and, at any realistic session length, never.

Her proposed fix does not work, and the reason is worth keeping: combining the two rungs as "date by size" or "size by date" is a lexicographic order, so whichever key goes first dominates and the second starves under a new name. Two fixes do work and only one avoids inventing a figure — a composite score needs weights, and a weight is a bare number with no derivation, which this project bans. Interleaving needs neither.

**Her amendment is what makes it a better design rather than a preference: order by age, never by length.** Entries gained 193, 344 and 472 words *while being processed* in one session, so ranking by line count means the entry ranked last can overtake the one ranked first with nobody touching the ladder. First-seen date cannot change. Length therefore survives only as membership in the long half, which is where its cost-of-reading justification actually sits.

A rung sits above the alternation on her second amendment: the entries both above the median line count and above the median age, oldest first. An intersection of two halves is roughly a quarter of the section — reachable inside one session, which is the whole reason it may sit there without starving what is beneath it. **Its stated justification is the opposite of the original hope, and is the stronger of the two:** an entry both long and old has been enriched across many sessions without resolving, so this rung reaches the work that keeps coming back, not the best-designed work. One counter-example is not a refutation and none is claimed.

**What the build found that the item had wrong.** The item asserted both medians were fields the digest already computes. Only the length median was. The age half had nothing to read, so the rung's second limb would have been worked out by hand — the judgment the ladder exists to remove. Raised with the user mid-build, and `queue_digest.py` was added to this item on her approval. `median_first_seen` drops entries whose date cannot be attributed rather than defaulting them, and takes the lower of the two middles for an even count rather than inventing a date between two real ones.

Checked against SPEC: its processing-flow paragraph already describes the intersection rung and the alternation, written in the planning session that kept this, so nothing was owed there.
