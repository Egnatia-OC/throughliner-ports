# [HASH] — Deepen /setup's interview into an adaptive, example-bank-driven discovery

The /setup interview was a fixed five questions. Made it freer, deeper, and adaptive — the motivation being interview quality/depth, not sparse output.

setup.md's Step 3 was rewritten from a fixed script into an adaptive discovery: Claude reads each answer and generates the next question that matters, drawing on an example-bank of topics (what/who, the core, principles, first thing to build, anything else) rather than reciting a list; recommends an answer to each; asks one at a time; and explores whatever material already exists first (a non-coder is often greenfield, but not always). The anti-overwhelm guard is a 5-Whys stopping rule — probe only to a shared buildable understanding — plus a stated "build from what we have" escape the user can call any time. Step 4's intro and two Rules bullets that referenced the old Q1–Q5 numbering were updated; Q6/Q7 stay fixed settings questions. The FAQ /setup entry was updated (dropping the stale "five short questions"). Design sharpened by web research (resources/research/setup-interview-depth-patterns.md).
