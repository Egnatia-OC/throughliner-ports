# a216873 — plan.md's provenance restatement cut to a pointer, and done.md's kept because it sits at the failure site

All four clauses of the always-loaded provenance rule — default-AI, the own-words bar, mixed authorship written as mixed, and the same bar binding reason-shaped sentences — were reproduced in `plan.md`'s capture-and-processing discipline and again inside `done.md`'s LOG entry template. The audit marked both as cite-and-restate: the pointer already works, so the restated body deletes with no loss.

**Only one of the two went, and the pairing is what decided it.** This project carries a live item, `[invented-rationale-compounds-past-the-shipped-rule]`, reporting that the shipped provenance rule is not holding in practice. The audit had no access to that evidence and could not have made this call.

**`done.md`'s copy stays because it sits exactly where the failure happens.** It is not a standalone restatement — it is three clauses inside the LOG entry template, at the moment a session re-authors an item's rationale into an entry. That re-authoring is the named failure site: the paired item records the rule being broken *with the rule loaded*. A rule restated at the keystroke where it is broken is doing different work from a rule restated in a doc's opening ground rules, and deleting the one copy positioned at the failure would have been the worst move available.

**`plan.md`'s copy went, minus one clause.** It reproduced all four canonical clauses hundreds of lines before any item is drafted. Its one genuinely new half — that the credit stays on the item after processing — survives as a short line pointing at the canonical rule.

**The "too much text" and "not working" readings do not actually conflict**, which is what removed the objection to cutting. The paired item's own evidence, from nine items processed on 2026-08-10, is that attribution *held* while volume grew. So the two failures are separable, and the provenance failure is not caused by a shortage of restatement. That is also why this cut is not offered as a fix for the paired item: it is not one.

**`done.md` was deliberately not touched by this item**, recorded here so a later pass does not finish the job.
