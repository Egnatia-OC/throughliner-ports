A planning chat that started with the user reporting she could not reach the law-prose restyle after days of trying, while the queue kept growing, and that she had responded by refusing to capture anything and keeping planning sessions as short as possible. Both levers turned out to be aimed at things that were not causing it.

**The queue-growth diagnosis, measured rather than argued.** 27 of the 60 unprocessed entries had been filed that day; roughly 20 of those were Claude's — filed at closes, mid-run, and from consumer projects' mail — against about 7 of hers. Across the whole section 44 of 60 were Claude's. So withholding her own captures reached about a quarter of the inflow at real cost to her, and shortening planning sessions works backwards, since processing and building are the only two things that remove entries.

**Why the restyle kept receding, which was neither of those.** It was fifth in the cleared region and nothing blocked it. The five preceding closes had spent their runs on a rename that carried a `Runs alone` marker and stopped after one item, a three-item run, an eleven-item hand conversion, and two planning sessions. Position was the whole problem, and it was a one-command fix.

**Claude then surfaced the forward advisory correctly and argued against it in the same session.** The advisory recommended planning before the next build run; Claude recommended closing and running the build without checking whether its reasons still held. The user caught it — *"The last session said to run plan though"*. Checking took one grep: one reason was dead, and the other was live and sat on the first two items of the run being recommended. Filed as [advisory-surfaced-then-overridden].

**The verbosity thread, which is the substantial half of this chat.** The user named Claude's verbosity as the single thing stopping her promoting the plugin anywhere but GitHub, with the YouTube channel waiting on it, and said she had been hoping the law-prose restyle would fix it as a side effect. It will not: that pass rewrites what Claude reads, and its acceptance test is a rule-statement count that cannot see a line of output.

**Claude then made the objection that nearly buried the whole thread, and the user defeated it in one sentence.** Claude cited SPEC's claim that rationale must ride a rule for the rule to be followed, and argued the restyle might therefore make narration rules fire less. Her reply — that this is from Opus 4.8 times — is correct, and the project's own research says so explicitly: Opus 5 *"doesn't need the heavy why-clause hardening"*, and carrying the why is *"partially obsolete as a compliance technique"*.

**Checking that correction turned up the finding this chat exists for.** The same two research files, live and unsuperseded since 2026-07-31, record that the current model family is **verbose by default**, narrates readily, and that **length is a prompt-side control** — and that a short brevity instruction steers as well as a quantified target or a list of patterns. SPEC meanwhile states that a prose instruction to be brief *"was tried first and measurably did nothing"*, which is the 4.8 result that caused word-count caps to be built. The caps were later retired and nothing replaced them, because the prose instruction was already marked tried-and-failed. **So the record talked this project out of the one lever its own current-model research recommends, for a whole model generation.** Both SPEC sentences were corrected here; the wider class is [spec-carries-4-8-era-claims].

**A sweep of the shipped docs found the defect is one clause**, not a missing rule: the always-loaded message-shape bullet ends *"at whatever length that takes"*, an explicit licence for any length on a model verbose by default, followed by a list of things not to do — which the wording rule names as the signal that the action was never specified.

**The user's two questions on the measurement improved it and are recorded because the objection is the useful part.** She asked whether a script measuring Claude's output would in effect create a cap. It would: a measurement with no standard becomes one the moment a session reads its median as a norm, which is the circularity SPEC already records against the retired bands. It was narrowed to a one-off instrument tied to its audit, with a stated delete-time under the temp-file rule, so availability cannot turn it into a standing measurement.
