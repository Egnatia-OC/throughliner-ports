**[inline-files-shadow-fix].** Tracing `_parse_build_files` confirmed the reported bug: it binds to the first line starting with `Files:`, so a stray inline `Files: a, b, c` line carried in from the batch's copied entry text shadows the real structured section, returns an empty list, and the hook denies every source edit — a build locked out of its own files. Fixed in two layers: harden the hook (load-bearing — robust to every future recurrence) and stop next.md copying the inline line in (authoring tidy-up). Alternative weighed and rejected: hook-only would have sufficed mechanically, but the belt-and-suspenders shape matches the spec-edit guard (mechanical guard + authoring discipline), and a clean working file is worth the small extra. The exact parser approach (bind-to-bare-header vs scan-all-sections) was left to build, with a note to pick the fail-safe one.
