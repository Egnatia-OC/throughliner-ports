# [HASH] — The no-length-caps position repealed and replaced with a per-shape bound derived from the project's own measured median

The build half of [cut-length-everywhere]; the planning entry of the same date carries the decision to repeal.

SPEC said no written shape carries a cap and the always-loaded standard said a record runs to whatever carries its facts — between them, an instruction to keep writing. That is why every earlier fix could only land on narration, which measures at roughly 19 words a message and was never the problem: one run talked 3,843 words and wrote 15,200 into the log.

Both statements are replaced by one bound. Each shape runs to the median of that shape in the project's own measured distribution, printed by `measure_written_shape_length.py`. A median is a proportion of the corpus rather than an invented figure, so it satisfies the stated-derivation rule, and it ratchets — writing to it pulls it down.

`plan.md` was cut 1,565 → 1,315 lines by deleting decision history and absorbing rationale into the rules it explained. Rule statements fell 328 → 319. Those nine were bold-led paragraphs that were pure explanation; naming them here is what keeps the fall an eviction rather than a silent loss, since `rule_signals.py` counts any bold-led paragraph as a rule.

The run halted partway on scope and the user's call was to capture the rest, so thirteen procedure docs and CLAUDE.md are uncut.
