**The length work was rebuilt twice, and the second rebuild was the user's.** [log-entry-length-may-be-mirrored] began as a measurement of LOG entry length. It ends as a measurement of four written shapes — captures, work items, entries and index lines — producing a two-tier word range derived from good specimens.

Getting there took two reversals. The first was Claude's: reading `done.md` established that nothing reads previous entry *bodies*, so both recorded instances of mirroring are index lines, and the user's proposal to rewrite recent entries would have changed a specimen nothing looks at. The second reversal was hers. Claude recommended specimen-first with a range as backstop, citing Anthropic's exemplar guidance and this project's local proof of it at `plan.md`'s checkpoint. She refused, on two grounds: a specimen is doc text in a corpus being actively shrunk, and it dictates appearance when only length was being decided. **The recommendation was withdrawn as mis-argued** — the exemplar evidence concerns *shape*, not length, so it never bore on the question. Her reasoning was better than the reasoning it displaced.

Two searches were run at her request and filed as `resources/research/llm-length-instruction-following.md`. Her friend's claim that models cannot write to a word count is correct and is LIFEBench's named root cause. But two findings cut her way: the documented failure direction under a length instruction is **undershoot**, which her own Discord posts reproduce exactly at ~75% of a 2,000-character limit every time; and the sharp deterioration sits at thousands of words, a regime none of this project's shapes occupy. She also asked whether ranges should be given in characters — the answer is no, and it is not close, since character counting runs near a 33% random baseline. Her "more sophisticated range" is the documented hard-versus-soft constraint pattern, and the action-on-breach is what answers this project's own repeal of the 20% index-line cap: advising is not refusing, so a tier that fires on correct work costs a moment's thought rather than blocking.

One flaw in the measurement as designed was caught before it shipped: the derivation method is measured *good* generations, and reading a band straight off a corpus whose whole problem is bloat would encode the bloat.
