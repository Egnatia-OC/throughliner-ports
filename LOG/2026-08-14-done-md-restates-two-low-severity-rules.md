Two low-rated audit findings, one accepted and one refused.

**The user-runnable table is refused, and the audit's own reservation carries the refusal.** Two rows splitting a user-only verification from a check Claude can run look like a restatement of the over-tag guard's "can Claude do this at all?" test. Replacing it with a pointer would send a session chasing the canonical guard to answer a question the notice exists to close. The audit rated this Low and said itself that a reasonable reading treats several of its Low findings as useful framing rather than redundancy; this is one of them, and two lines is not a cost worth the loss.
