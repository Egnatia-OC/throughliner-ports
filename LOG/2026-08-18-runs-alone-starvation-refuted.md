# 02ec308 — the starvation claim is refuted and the item becomes a digest line, with its own proposed fix rejected

The item said a `Runs alone` item placed last could be overtaken indefinitely and never reached. Checked rather than accepted: it was filed about [rename-to-throughliner] receding, and that shipped three days later. A `Runs alone` item is not deferred indefinitely by work sitting ahead of it either — /next stops **before** it, so the following run reaches it. Genuine starvation needs planning to outpace building forever, which is a throughput problem rather than a placement one. The heading said "never reached" and was rewritten.

**What survives is visibility.** This session placed six items ahead of the `docs-b` rename and the queue gave no sign of it; it was said out loud only because Claude happened to notice, which is the siteless-check failure this project has recorded five times.

**Which is why the item's own third option was refused.** It floated /plan *narrating* the placement — exactly what happened today, unprompted and therefore unrepeatable. A fresh short session, which this method designs for, would not notice.

The fix is mechanical and needs no invented number: `queue_digest.py` prints one more computed line, how many cleared items sit ahead of each `Runs alone` item. A count is reportable where an age is not — how long something has been cleared would need a threshold nobody can derive, while what sits in front of it is arithmetic. The disposition records that a mechanical check replaced a prose rule, which is this project's own test for retiring a noticing.
