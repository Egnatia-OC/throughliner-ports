# [HASH] — Write-first rule: two rationale clauses evicted, the consent clause reclassified as operative

Found by the compliance audit's fourth lens, the delete-and-read test. The
always-loaded write-first rule had grown two clauses that read as explanation
rather than instruction, and because this is the file loaded at every session
start, their weight is paid by every session in every project.

The split was settled at planning by the caveat the audit itself raised. "Consent
happens in conversation, in plain words, before the write" is operative: delete it
and a session could read write-first as consent-free, which is the misreading the
sentence exists to prevent. The other two — that the file is the record of what was
agreed rather than where agreement happens, and that showing recoverable text
before writing it costs a wait that buys nothing — leave a complete instruction
behind when cut, so they are rationale by the test's own definition.

Nothing was relocated. The why was authored deliberately in
`2026-08-26-audit-write-first-contradiction.md`, which already holds it, so this
build cites that entry rather than writing the history anywhere new.
