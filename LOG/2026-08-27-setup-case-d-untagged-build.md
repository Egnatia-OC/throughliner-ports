# [HASH] — setup.md's tag-free declaration repealed and every step tagged

/setup used to declare itself free of response-shape tags, on the reasoning that
it runs on two kinds of session — a fresh adoption where the rules defining those
tags are not loaded, and a migration inside an adopted project where they are —
and that one text cannot carry markers meaning something on one run and nothing
on the other.

The declaration lost to the editing habit that kept violating it: seven tags had
accumulated in the doc anyway. Honouring it would have meant stripping those,
and a label is harmless where it goes unread. So the declaration comes out and
the doc says instead that each step's prose carries its behaviour in full, with
the tags as a summary of it — which makes both kinds of session work without
either depending on the tags.

Case D was the section that prompted this: it carried no tags at all while two
of its steps wait on the user. Its subpart clarifier and its pop-out mail draft
now carry `[PROMPT]`, and the rest of the doc was reviewed to match, with each
conditional arm written with its condition outside the brackets.

**Files touched:** `plugin/throughliner/docs/setup.md` — frontmatter note, the
opening declaration, and tags on Step 0, Step 1, Case D's two waiting steps,
Step 2C and its sub-steps, Step 2 and its INBOX line, Step 3 and Step 4. The
seven existing tags were kept as they were.
