# 4c9b5e8 — First /plan after the emergency revert: six recoveries cleared, the authoring heuristic redesigned into an admission-and-eviction gate, and the revert's stale-premise pattern turned into a sweep

The 2026-08-09 emergency revert took the working tree back to 2026-08-02, unwinding 65 commits carrying 214 shipped items. This session was the first planning pass over the queue afterwards, and its dominant finding was structural rather than any single item: **seven times, a queue item asked for work the pre-revert history had already done.** The editing-state signal a companion project depends on, docset B's twelve fidelity findings and their repairs, the four-part `[user]`-line design, the Claude Code feedback direction, the progressive-disclosure decision, the outbound-correspondence rule, and `self-authoring-rules.md` itself — each found by accident, when an item's premise turned out to be stale. Finding them one accident at a time is not a method, so [pre-revert-recovery-sweep] replaces it with one pass over the LOG index and the 31 designed-but-unbuilt queue items the revert also discarded.

The session's other half was the revert's cause. `resources/authoring-heuristic.md` was in force throughout the growth of plugin-behaviour.md from 6,162 to 21,445 words — it was applied, not skipped. Reading it against that outcome showed why: six of its seven checks either add text to each rule or explicitly resist cutting, so every addition passed honestly and the file grew anyway; and none of the seven asks whether a rule should exist at all, or what comes out to make room. It is a quality filter with no admission control and no eviction policy. Research confirmed the diagnosis and reframed the target: the binding limit is a **count of instructions** (roughly 150–200) rather than a word count, irrelevant content causes **wholesale dismissal of all instructions** rather than selective filtering, and repository analysis finds negative correlation with performance past 1,000 words — putting plugin-behaviour.md six times past that threshold at its *birth*. The revert bought room, not health.

A second research pass, at the user's direction, borrowed from legal drafting and supplied what the first could not. Legislation names the distinction the method needed — **amendment versus freestanding provision** — which maps exactly onto the instruction ceiling: a freestanding rule burns a slot, an amendment burns none. It also supplied the eviction mechanism nothing in the AI-authoring literature covers: **recasting**, repeal-and-replace with a single clean text when amendments have accreted past legibility. And it surfaced a fork that turned out to already be settled: legislation never puts reasoning in operative text, and the project's own 5-series research says the same thing — "keep the why-pipeline; stop treating why-attachment as a compliance requirement." Two independent lines reaching one split, never applied. Rationale now moves to the LOG; the operative rule stays bare.

Decisions and their losing alternatives, recorded so they are not relitigated. The ceiling stays **qualitative** — no numeric rule budget. Of the old seven checks, only *action, not prohibition* survives. Lost work that arrived as prose rules is **re-authored leaner**, not restored verbatim and not dropped. Two restorations were deliberately refused: the CLAUDE.md rule declaring the monolithic behaviour file deliberate, because the user identified that the recurrence comes from *them*, not from Claude, so a rule aimed at Claude targets the wrong party and repeats the always-loaded bloat that forced the revert; and the resume-from-pause procedure step, because Claude is frozen between turns and cannot detect a pause at all, so the fix attached to a moment that does not exist. A proposal to recover shipped work by restoring processed queue items was checked and could not work — a shipped item's queue text is consumed when it builds — but its principle survived as the better rule: prose is never restored as a diff, only as a work item that must pass the new gate.

Also done: 17 research and testing files restored from the pre-revert tree, including three executable test files, all zero-bloat by construction. A harness issue was filed upstream as anthropics/claude-code#85084, on the mid-turn message injection instructing the model to fold a new topic into the running turn. And a correspondence exchange with a companion project corrected three claims in both directions and produced the field semantics the editing-signal recovery now builds against — including that a version/path mismatch, not version 1 itself, is the silent failure worth a test.
