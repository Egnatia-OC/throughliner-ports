# dc52025 — the blocker field accepts several slugs, and the fifteen-file ripple was accepted knowingly

Work waiting on a *group* of items had no way to say so, so it could not be held at all — and a proxy blocker is worse than none, because a field that resolves early produces a wrong lift, and a lift is a clearing move that sends the item into an unattended run.

Of the item's three shapes, the first was taken: `Blocked by:` accepts several slugs and the item lifts only when all resolve. It extends a field that exists rather than adding a mechanism; the lint already parses one slug and the revisit already asks one question per held item.

The named-group shape was refused as a new state with its own lifecycle, which is the failure this method has caught repeatedly. The do-nothing shape was refused too, and it was the tempting one because it costs nothing: leaving group-gated work in Unprocessed misfiles designed, buildable work as a capture, and reading a wait out of prose is exactly what the field was built to replace — recorded in the item itself, where one item sat shelved for weeks because its wait was a sentence nobody could see as work.

The ripple was traced by grep before the file list was written, as a hook-enforced format change requires: about fifteen live sites across the lint, the digest, the dependency facts, the mover, four procedure docs, both FAQs, SPEC, CLAUDE.md and three suites.

That cost was stated plainly and the user accepted it, on the ground that the alternative is work which cannot be held at all. Claude's framing of the cost — that it was "much larger than the problem that raised it" — was itself a small breach of the rule against caveats that argue against doing a thing rather than describing it, and she caught it.

Rule gate: run — a widening of the existing `Blocked by:` field. Two alternatives refused outright.
