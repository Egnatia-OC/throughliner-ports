# 7e3c1c8 — the band applies to the entry as it stands, not to the paragraph being added

The word band is restated to govern the item **as it stands** rather than the text being added, and the read is sited at /plan's keep-step, where the item is already in front of you.

The gap was that bands govern the moment text is written. An item can pass its band at capture, be enriched at five successive planning sessions, and reach the build at three times its ceiling without any single write breaching anything — every author in band, the artifact not. Measured: items first filed in August grew a median of 145 words between capture and Processed, with a maximum of 4,425. July's median growth was zero, so the accretion is recent.

The keep-step's existing band read partly covered this already, but only where a session rewrites the whole block rather than appending a paragraph to it — and appending is the common move.

Demonstrated in the session that settled it: every item processed that day was extended by appending a settlement paragraph, no single write came near a band, and several items passed their ceiling.

The clause landed in two places, which is one rule at its statement site and its firing site: the bands block in the always-loaded rules says what the band applies to, and `plan.md`'s keep-step says to read the item rather than the addition, because that is the one moment the whole item is in view.
