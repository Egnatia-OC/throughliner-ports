# 7e3c1c8 — the write is held while a design is still being worked out

One clause on the write-first rule: while a design or a disposition is still being worked out, offer to capture and hold the write until the user says go. It covers ideation in any skill and the processing of captures in /plan alike, since both are the same shape — text being worked out rather than finished.

The rule's own test is untouched. Its question — is the previous version recoverable without the user's help? — holds. What it assumed silently is that the text is *finished*, and mid-ideation it is not, so it fired at every turn of a design that had not settled. Six writes across one continuous discussion, three on each of two captures, each a full re-authoring of text nobody had finished. Those two came to 634 and 586 words against a ceiling of 265; the one held and written once came to 354.

Read as direction rather than proof, which the item insisted on: one held instance is one data point, it still breaches the ceiling by a third, and the two long items genuinely absorbed more content across those turns.

The objection — a chat ending unexpectedly loses the design — is answered by the close's re-scan and /rescan, which reach a held design like anything else decided and never written. Neither needed rewording to say so.

Rule gate: run — a clause on the existing write-first rule; no slot spent, nothing evicted.
