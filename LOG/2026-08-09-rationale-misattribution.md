# [HASH] — A `captured by you` credit now requires the user's own words as its source, mixed authorship is written as mixed, and the same bar binds reason-shaped sentences in the prose

This item exists because of a correction the user made in discussion, and the failure is theirs to state: it is weird having Claude repeatedly credit you with saying and deciding things Claude itself said and decided. The record accuses you of authorship. Claude's initial assumption was different and wrong — that a misattributed why causes a bad decision downstream — and no instance of that could be found. The real cost is immediate and felt, not hypothetical and later. Recording the rejected framing matters here, because it is the intuitive one and would otherwise be re-proposed.

Three instances in the session that discussed it. Every work item written that session carried "Captured by you," including this item's own predecessor and `[below-line-revisit-nags-by-another-route]`, which Claude narrowed almost entirely from its own reading of the shipped docs — the user approved it, they did not reason it. And on `[retire-docset-a]` Claude wrote "Their reason: both Fable 5 and Opus 5 now work," when what the user said was that both work now and it's fine. The reason-shaped sentence is Claude's, worn as theirs.

This is not a new rule, and treating it as one would have been the wrong move. The provenance convention already said the credit tracks who *stood behind* the item and that when in doubt it stays unmarked. Claude marked in doubt three times in one session. Under the authoring gate that makes it admission test 4 — a rule that slipped needs a hook or sharper wording, never a second rule beside it — so nothing new was admitted and no instruction slot was consumed. A hook was ruled out on the evidence rather than left open: the lint sees only text, and nothing in a finished work item distinguishes reasoning the session produced from reasoning the user gave, so there is no signal to match on. Sharper wording was the only available route.

The wording is built on an exemplar the user brought, from a capture authored in their Understudy project: *"Bundling once by hand and committing the flattened file was rejected on Claude's recommendation and Alex's agreement."* That names who did which part instead of assigning the whole to one party, and it is the shape the rule now asks for. Three clauses came out of it. A `captured by you` credit requires the user's own words as its source — not their approval, since agreeing to a proposal Claude reasoned out is agreement, not authorship. Mixed authorship is written as mixed, naming the roles. And the same bar binds reasoning *inside* the prose — "their reason", "the user's call", "on their instruction" — which is where the damage actually lands, because the credit line is at least a known convention while a reason-shaped sentence reads as testimony.

The rule ships with its own why attached, deliberately: `captured by you` reads as generous and costs nothing to type, which is exactly why it slips, and a rule whose failure mode is "it feels kind" needs the cost named where the writing happens.

One framing is retired rather than deferred. This item began as proactive rationale-nudging — Claude asking for the *why* at decision moments. It was dropped: it would add an always-loaded judgement-based ask against a 150–200 instruction ceiling, its failure mode is the nag the method has spent weeks removing, and the real problem turned out to be misattribution rather than under-capture. `[throughline-value-articulation]` and `[rename-to-throughliner]` should not expect a nudging design to arrive.

The three items misattributed that day were corrected here. All three — `[cross-project-inbox]`, `[retire-docset-a]`, `[below-line-revisit-nags-by-another-route]` — were in this same run, so they had already left QUEUE.md for `_build.md` by the time this item was built. The corrections were made in their `_build.md` entry text instead, which is what each item's LOG entry was then authored from, so the corrected attribution is what reached the permanent record rather than the queue copy that no longer exists.
