# 47966bb — `[DISCUSS]` is redefined as a two-way exchange in short turns rather than the one tag that licenses length

Raised by Alex on 2026-08-13 in her own words — "delivering a lecture is not
discussing" — after a keep-step ran to several hundred words on an item she could
decide in two lines. The wording fix is Claude's.

**The defect was that the definition legally outvoted the concision work.** The
tag was defined as "engage substantively — tradeoffs, concerns, a recommendation.
The one tag that licenses length." That last sentence reads as permission to
expound, and the tags' own precedence rule says procedure tags govern during a
skill — so the output style shipped hours earlier was not broken here, it was
outvoted by a definition inviting the opposite behaviour.

**The replacement names the shape rather than the size:** a two-way exchange, in
short turns with the user answering between them, so depth arrives as more
back-and-forth rather than as one longer message.

**Rule gate: run** — a reword of an existing definition. No rule added, none
evicted, no slot consumed.
