**Posted announcement (Discord, 2026-08-08), full text:**

> **Something new on the drawing board: learning mode.**
>
> There are two things about vibe coding I keep noticing that nobody's really saying out loud — not because it's a secret, it's just how things are.
>
> First: a lot of people with app ideas aren't ready to hear "you don't need to learn to code." Some of us have held onto the idea of one day learning for years, and it's not something you just drop because a tool came along. Second, the thing nobody's necessarily telling those people: most working developers aren't really reading the code line-by-line anymore either. Meanwhile half of YouTube is still yelling that if you don't understand every line of your app it's going to explode.
>
> So the plan: at setup, one question — **do you want to learn to code?** Either way you get real terminology with real explanations, and everything explained lands in a glossary that remembers what you've already been told — so Claude never re-explains what you know, and never assumes you know what it hasn't told you. The difference is that Claude knows which kind of person it's working with. Say no, and explanations stay light and there's never any homework — you can be re-told what a thing means as many times as you like, no shame in it. Say yes, and Claude leans in: more depth, and you're invited to ask about anything unfamiliar.
>
> Honestly, everyone is learning all the time either way — that's kind of the point. This isn't about gating learning off from anyone; it's about providing a little extra for the people who explicitly want it.
>
> Interestingly, the research backs this up: Anthropic's own study found AI-assisted learners understood *less* — unless they used the AI to ask why things work rather than just accepting generated code, in which case they beat the no-AI group. That's the learner the mode is being designed around.
>
> Still in design — the glossary is further along and coming first. Thoughts welcome, especially from anyone in the "I still want to learn" camp.
