# 8330209 — the ordering ladder rebuilt so decay is reachable, with length deciding membership and never order

The interleave had been adopted for one session as a live trial, and the capture said it needed checking against a real queue before it could be kept. This session was the second check and reproduced the defect from the other direction: unblock-potential was worked to exhaustion, the order fell to longest-first, and the decay rung was never approached — the above-median group stood at roughly fifteen entries and a handful were processed. The oldest entry was reached, and not by the ladder: it surfaced because a pattern was noticed across three deferrals, which is the siteless-noticing failure recorded five times here. It worked, and it is not repeatable.

Alex amended the design twice and both amendments are better than what they replaced. First, order the above-median set by **oldest first** rather than by length. The argument is that line count is not a stable key: entries gained 193, 344 and 472 words *while being processed* in this session, so longest-first ranks by something that mutates as the work happens, and the entry ranked last can overtake the one ranked first with nobody touching the ladder. First-seen date cannot change. Length therefore stops deciding order anywhere and survives only as membership in the long half. Second, a rung above the alternation: work first the entries **both** longer and older than the section's middle, oldest first, and begin alternating when that set is empty. It is an intersection of two medians, each a proportion the digest already computes, each fixed at the opening — derivable rather than judged, and bounded, since two overlapping halves make roughly a quarter of the section against the old rung's half that was never once emptied.

Her question about recomputing membership as entries grow was answered no, and not on cost: recomputation is one command, but an entry swelling past the median mid-session would re-enter a group already worked past, the group would stop shrinking, and a rung that never yields makes everything beneath it unreachable — the exact defect being repaired.

Two things were refused with their reasons. Combining the rungs lexicographically, as date-by-size or size-by-date, only relabels the starvation, since one key dominates and the other starves under a new name. A composite score needs weights, and a weight is a bare number with no derivation.

Alex's recovered intent for longest-first — reaching the best-designed entries first — was tested rather than adopted. The counter-example sits in the queue: the longest and one of the older entries is the single one that provably cannot be kept, on its third skip, because at the top of the distribution length tracks repeated enrichment without resolution. She accepted the point. So the rung's honest justification is that it reaches the work that keeps coming back, not the work that is well designed. One counter-example is not a refutation and none is claimed; both halves are recorded so a later session tests it rather than rediscovering either.

She accepted the dilution as the price of elevating a rung that was never reached. The rung depends on captures being able to bow out, which is `2026-08-19-not-before-reaches-unprocessed.md`.
