# e5d169b — Adding a scan to a skill opening must state what it displaces

A skill opening is the one place the method accretes without any addition looking like a rule: each scan is individually small, individually tagged, and folded into one consolidated narration, so nothing counts them. /plan's Step 1 now fires eight, and the most recent was added by the session that noticed, with nothing objecting. A rule permitting unlimited additions so long as each is small has no stopping condition.

`resources/self-authoring-rules.md` §2 (Eviction) gains one amendment: adding a scan to a skill's opening states what it displaces. No cap and no number — any figure would be invented, which the derivation rule bans, and each part being individually bounded is exactly what fails to bound the sum. Naming the displaced scan is what forces the sum to be looked at.

**The home correction was the substance of this item, not a detail.** It was filed proposing the rule live in `plan.md`. Nothing in `plugin/si-plugin/docs-b/` changed: `plan.md` is the document being protected, not the one needing the rule. Adding a scan is *authoring* work, and consumers never author scans, so a rule shipped in `plan.md` would fire for every consumer planning session forever to govern an act that happens a handful of times a year in one project. Written as an amendment to the eviction question it is a special case of, it consumes no always-loaded slot.

**The evidence is recorded at its honest strength.** The item cited the opening growing by one scan with nothing objecting, which is true, but the realised harm is a single instance — [advisory-read-but-not-surfaced], where the advisory was read at Step 1 and not surfaced for three hours. The /plan that processed this item ran the same opening and dropped nothing. So the supported claim is "it has happened once and the opening keeps growing", not "it happens routinely", and the weaker claim is what the rule's text carries — the stronger one would not survive checking.
