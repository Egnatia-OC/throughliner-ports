# [HASH] — After post-close work that changed a file, the close offers to append it to the record

`done.md`'s Recommend next gained the offer: where further work after the close
changes a file, offer once to append a marked tail section to this session's LOG
entry.

The user's observation, in her words: she has been routinely asking Claude to
amend the log for a session after post-close work, and wondered whether it could
be built in as an offer. The recurrence is the evidence — this is not one missed
step but a request she has had to make repeatedly, which is the same signal that
carried the reply-draft offer. The work happened, the record does not show it, and
the only mechanism closing the gap was her remembering.

What makes the region distinctive: /done writes the entry and commits it, so
anything afterwards is real work with a finished record already committed against
it. The session has ended in the method's terms and not in the chat's.

Three decisions, the reasoning Claude's and agreed. **Append, never rewrite** —
the entry is committed by then, so amending its prose obscures what was recorded
at the close; a marked tail shows plainly that the work came afterwards, and the
index line is left alone unless the tail changes what the entry is about. **Once
per tail, not per exchange** — an offer reappearing after every message is the
nagging shape the method keeps deleting, the same defect as the checkpoint recital
and the close's /next invitation. **Only where a file changed** — post-close
conversation that alters nothing has nothing to record, and offering there trains
the user to decline.

Kept separate rather than folded into its two neighbours, reversing a call made
twice earlier the same day, so the reason is recorded: those folds were items
rewriting the same paragraph, and these three are not. More decisively, this is
small and buildable now while `[done-delta-close]` is large and undesigned —
folding the small into the large makes it hostage to a design nobody has done.
It also answers a question that item left open: what prompts a cheap post-close
close is neither a mode nor auto-detection, but Claude offering.

`done-build.md` was checked rather than assumed and needs nothing: its Phase 3
runs `done.md`'s Recommend next, where the offer now lives.
